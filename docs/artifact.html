Our Mission

Love, compassion and unity — shown, not just spoken.

Our mission is rooted in the core teachings of the Word of God, where love, compassion and unity are shown, inspired by the teachings and life of our loving Lord Jesus Christ. We are a dedicated non-profit organization committed to making a positive impact on the lives of those in need in the Philippines.

Our Vision

Hope, dignity and a bright future for all.

At Jesus Oneness Love Mission, we believe in the power of the Holy Spirit by spreading the Word of God to transform mind, body and soul. Our charity is driven by a singular purpose: to spread the love and kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ to all, regardless of their background or circumstances. We aim to provide support and resources to the less fortunate, helping them find hope, dignity and a bright future.

What we do

Six ministries, one household at a time.

The work is not abstract. It is a chair for a neighbour who cannot walk, a hospital bill paid, a Tuesday evening opening Scripture in someone's living room.

01

Bible Study

Weekly gatherings in homes and barangay halls, opening the Word together for youth, ushers and families.

Weekly · Sto. Tomas

02

Kids Ministry & DVBS

Daily Vacation Bible School, thanksgiving parties and programs built so a child is treated as a beam of sunlight.

Seasonal · Ages 4–12

03

Medical Assistance

Hospitalization support and medicine for families who would otherwise go without care.

On request · Batangas

04

Wheelchair Sponsorship

Mobility restored to neighbours living with disability, sponsored chair by chair.

Ongoing · Nationwide

05

Jail Ministry

Visiting those inside, because the Lord remembers the prisoner as surely as the free.

Monthly · Local facilities

06

Community Outreach

Relief and rebuilding for homeless families, including our ongoing work in Caingin, Sta. Rosa, Laguna.

As needed · Luzon
